# Pilot exercise — HomeLab Recovery Kit

We need feedback from people who maintain a Proxmox VE, Docker Compose or NAS setup. The pilot is voluntary, unpaid and takes about 45–60 minutes. A selected tester receives the complete pre-sale review candidate for evaluation. We are testing usability and demand, not asking for a favorable review.

## Before opening the tool

Think of one real outage or restore that would be painful in your setup. You can describe the problem in broad terms; do not share your actual network diagram, hostnames, keys or backups.

## Tasks

1. Use **fictional** names and addresses to represent a small part of your setup: at least two systems, their dependency and where their backups would be found.
2. Work without assistance for up to 45 minutes. Make a recovery plan, export it and inspect the printable view.
3. Note where you got stuck, which missing dependency the tool helped expose and what it failed to show.
4. After seeing the complete kit, tell us candidly whether you would buy it today at **€24.90**, and what would need to change. “No” is useful feedback.

We will record completion time, whether the plan was useful and any critical errors. No screen recording or personal configuration is required. Do not upload your JSON or PDF to a public issue. You may stop at any time. If you volunteer through a public GitHub issue, your username and comments are public; arrange any private contact outside the issue.
